# Mise: software first

The interface is the product. Keep everyday changes simple, touch controls easy to reach, and edits intact as Edge resizes.

## Implemented

- Browser-local food and tool inventory; separate purchase batches and manual correction.
- Fridge/freezer/pantry groups, entered expiration dates, unknown quantities, and stocked/low/empty staples.
- Barcode review with remembered corrections, default automatic continuation, and deliberate repeat confirmation.
- In-app autocomplete, quick shopping additions, countable −1 with Undo, and larger quantity edits.
- TheMealDB starter suggestions; editable personal recipes and pasted-text imports.
- Conservative quantity matching, selected ingredients, and optional expiration priority.
- Recipe steps, timers, optional wake lock, and confirmed post-cooking deductions.
- Saved meal plans and shopping lists generated on request, with inventory subtracted from combined requirements.
- Optional authenticated Cloudflare backend for free-tier Gemini chat, text/small-media extraction, and public Recipe JSON-LD imports.
- Full kitchen backups that exclude credentials, old-format restore, and Markdown copying.
- Same-URL updates without forced reload.

## Later

Device sync, Obsidian integration, receipt import, inventory history, opened/thawed dates, voice, comprehensive product/recipe catalogues, store-specific brand/price research, and natural-language inventory edit proposals remain future work. Keep manual entry and the copy-to-Gemini fallback available.

## Hardware

The eventual independent fridge client uses the same website in Edge. Hardware selection comes later: portrait touch display on the magnetic freezer door, about 24 by 18 inches of usable space, approximately 16-inch preference, white/removable mounting, roughly one inch total depth, and $150 target. The software makes no claim that a particular screen or mount fits.
